DUX Lab @ MSU

The Design & User Experience Lab is a student-faculty collaborative that designs and builds user interfaces for academic projects, experiential learning engagements—and sometimes just for fun.

Work with the DUX Lab

Our Approach

Comprehensive, evidence-led, built in public.

We treat every project as a small piece of a larger system — a product, a team, a community — and design accordingly.

Most design work optimizes a single screen or flow in isolation. DUX Lab starts from the opposite end: we map the people, incentives, and constraints around a problem before we touch a single pixel. The result is work that holds up once it leaves the prototype and meets real users, real data, and real edge cases.

Every engagement produces two things — a shipped artifact, and a public write-up of what we learned, so the next team doesn't have to start from zero.

  • Research-first
  • Systems-oriented
  • Inclusive by default
  • Open by default
  • Built for maintainability
