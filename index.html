About the DUX Lab

Experiential learning through design.

The DUX Lab crafts human-friendly design and user experiences in an increasingly AI-dominated world.

The Design and User Experience Lab brings industry process and experience to an academic setting. Our primary goal is to give students at Michigan State University opportunities to work on real-world projects across interdisciplinary teams.

DUX is lead by Media & Information Professor of Practice Jeff Siarto and a team of undergraduate and graduate students across a variety of majors at MSU. Our lab can work with academic units around campus, commercial and non-profit groups outside the university, and actively participates in MSU's Vertically Integrated Projects program.
